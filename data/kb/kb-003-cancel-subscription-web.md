# How to cancel a subscription bought on the website

Subscriptions bought on nebula web checkout are managed by Nebula directly.

Steps:
1. Log in at the Nebula website with the email you used at checkout.
2. Go to Account > Subscription.
3. Click Cancel subscription and confirm.

You will receive a confirmation email within 15 minutes. If you do not see the Account page, contact support from the email address used for the purchase and we will cancel it for you. Access continues until the end of the paid period.
