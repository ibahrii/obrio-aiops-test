# Problems with chat or video calls

If a chat or video call does not start, freezes or drops:
1. Update Nebula to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Check that the app has permission to use the camera and microphone.
3. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data; video calls need a stable connection of at least 2 Mbps.
4. Log out and log back in.

If the session was interrupted because of a technical problem on our side, the unused minutes are returned as credits after support reviews the session log.
When reporting a bug, include your device model, OS version, app version and the time of the issue.
