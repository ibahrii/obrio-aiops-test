# How to choose an expert

Each expert profile shows their specialisation (astrology, tarot, numerology and others), languages, rating, number of sessions and price per minute in credits.
Use the filters on the Experts tab to select a topic such as career, love or family, and a language.
Many experts offer a free first 3 minutes so you can check whether their style suits you.
Read recent reviews on the profile before booking.
