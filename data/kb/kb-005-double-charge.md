# I was charged twice

A second charge usually has one of three causes: a temporary authorization hold that disappears within 3–5 business days; two separate purchases (for example a subscription and a credit pack); or a genuine duplicate charge.

What support does:
1. Ask for the date, amount and the last 4 digits of the card, or a screenshot of the bank statement.
2. Check the payment history in the admin panel.
3. If a genuine duplicate charge is confirmed, the duplicate is refunded in full regardless of the 14-day policy.

Duplicate charges are handled as P1 because of the risk of a chargeback.
