# The app crashes or does not load

If Nebula crashes on launch or a screen does not load:
1. Restart your device.
2. Update the app to the latest version.
3. Clear the app cache (Android: Settings > Apps > Nebula > Storage > Clear cache).
4. Reinstall the app and log in with the same account; purchases are restored automatically.

If the app is completely unusable for a paying customer after these steps, support escalates the case to the Tech team as P1 and collects device model, OS version and app version.
