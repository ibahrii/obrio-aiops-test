# Can't log in or reset my password

If you cannot log in:
1. Make sure you use the same login method as when you signed up (email, Apple or Google). Accounts created with Sign in with Apple must be accessed with Apple.
2. Tap Forgot password on the login screen. The reset email is valid for 1 hour.
3. If the reset email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check the Spam and Promotions folders and add support@nebula.example to your contacts.

If the email still does not arrive, support can verify your identity and trigger a manual reset. Support never asks for your password.
