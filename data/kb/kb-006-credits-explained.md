# How credits work

Credits are the in-app currency used for chats and calls with experts. Each expert sets a price per minute in credits, which is shown on their profile before you start a session.
Purchased credits do not expire while your account is active.
Bonus credits received from promotions expire 30 days after they are granted.
Credits are tied to your Nebula account, not to a device, so you can use them on iOS, Android and the web after logging in with the same account.
Unused credits stay on your balance if you change or cancel your subscription.
