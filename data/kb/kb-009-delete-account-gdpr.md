# Deleting your account and personal data (GDPR)

You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. Deletion requests and data access (export) requests are handled by the Privacy team and are completed within 30 days, as required by GDPR.
Before deletion, active subscriptions must be cancelled; deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription.
Deletion is permanent: chat history, readings and unused credits cannot be restored.
Support agents do not delete data themselves. They confirm the request, verify the account owner and forward the case to the Privacy team.
