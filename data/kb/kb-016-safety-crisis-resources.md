# Safety and crisis situations

Nebula provides entertainment and self-reflection content and is not a substitute for professional mental health care.
If a customer mentions self-harm, suicidal thoughts, or being in danger, the agent must respond with care, must not give astrological or tarot advice about the situation, and must encourage the customer to contact local emergency services or a crisis line immediately.
International directories of crisis lines are available at findahelpline.com.
In the United States, people can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). In the UK and Ireland, Samaritans are available on 116 123.
Every such ticket is escalated to the Trust & Safety team immediately, even if the customer also asks about billing or other topics.
