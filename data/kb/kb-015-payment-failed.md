# My payment does not go through

If a payment fails:
1. Check that the card has enough funds and is enabled for online and international payments.
2. Make sure the billing address and postal code match the card.
3. Try another card or PayPal on the web checkout.

Some banks block the first payment to a new merchant; in this case confirm the payment in your banking app and try again.
Support cannot see full card details and cannot complete a payment on your behalf.
