# How we review complaints about experts

Every complaint about an expert is reviewed by the Expert Quality team within 3 business days. The team reviews the session transcript or recording and the expert's history.
Possible outcomes include a warning, additional training, temporary suspension or removal of the expert from the platform.
If the review confirms that the session violated our quality standards, the credits spent on that session can be returned.
We do not share the outcome of disciplinary actions against a specific expert with the customer, but we always confirm that the review is completed.
Serious accusations (fraud, insults, harassment, requests to move off the platform) are escalated immediately.
