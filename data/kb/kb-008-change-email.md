# How to change the email on my account

To change your email address, open Profile > Settings > Account > Email, enter the new address and confirm it from the link we send to the new inbox.
If you no longer have access to the old email, support can change it after verifying ownership of the account with the last 4 digits of the payment card and the approximate date of the first purchase.
