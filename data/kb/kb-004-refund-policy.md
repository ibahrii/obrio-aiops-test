# Refund policy

Web purchases (subscriptions and credit packs bought on the Nebula website) can be refunded within 14 days of the charge if the purchased credits or premium features have not been used.
A refund for a partially used credit pack covers only the unused credits.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google under their own rules; Nebula support cannot issue those refunds directly.
Requests outside the 14-day window, or for used services, are reviewed individually by a support specialist and are not guaranteed.
Agents must not promise a refund before the eligibility check is completed.
