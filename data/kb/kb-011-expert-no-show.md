# The expert did not show up for my session

If an expert did not join a scheduled session, the credits reserved for that session are returned automatically within 24 hours.
If the credits were not returned after 24 hours, support can return them manually after checking the session log.
Repeated no-shows by the same expert are reported to the Expert Quality team.
You can book another expert immediately; your returned credits can be used for any expert.
