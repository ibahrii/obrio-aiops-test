# Support tone of voice (internal guide)

Internal guide for agents.
Always reply in the customer's language.
Acknowledge the customer's feelings in one sentence, then move to concrete steps. Avoid exaggerated phrases such as "we are terribly, deeply sorry".
Never blame the customer and never argue about whether astrology works.
Do not promise refunds, compensation or exceptions unless they are confirmed by policy or by a team lead.
Do not use emojis in formal replies.
Sign replies as "Nebula Support Team".
